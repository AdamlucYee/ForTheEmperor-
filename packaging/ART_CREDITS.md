# Artwork and runtime credits / 素材来源

This is an unofficial Warhammer 40,000 fan project. No Games Workshop licence or endorsement has been obtained. Warhammer-related names, chapter designs and other intellectual property remain subject to their respective owners' rights.

The eight character sprite atlases were generated using ImageGen from text prompts describing chapter colours, armour, weapons and poses. They are AI-generated images, not hand-drawn original character designs. Prompts are recorded in `assets/sprites/generation-prompts.json` in the source repository.

Visual-direction research included:

- Ignacio Polo Garzón — [Space marine](https://ignaciopolo.artstation.com/projects/V2LN2n)
- DaTico — [In the grim darkness of the far future, there is only pixel art](https://www.newgrounds.com/art/view/datico/in-the-grim-darkness-of-the-far-future-there-is-only-pixel-art)

Those artists' images were not provided as inputs to the final atlas generation calls and are not bundled as application artwork. Attribution does not imply their permission, participation or endorsement. AI generation does not grant rights to the underlying franchise designs.

Sprite playback, movement and dynamic particles are implemented by the application. File icons come from Windows Shell. Optional sounds use Windows system sounds; there is no character voice acting.

The executable embeds Microsoft .NET and Windows Desktop runtimes. Their licence files and applicable third-party notices are included in `licenses` and apply to their respective components, not to the Warhammer artwork or branding.
